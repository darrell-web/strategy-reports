Risk Watch — Kota Canna Recovery Curve

Kota Canna Dispensary (76 reviews, 4.9★, Plainfield) is currently dominating cannabis + dispensary terms via Google's new-business boost (90+ SoLV across all 4 terms). The boost typically decays in 6–12 months. We're tracking three signals:

  • Kota Canna review velocity — if reviews are accumulating fast, the boost may extend. If slow, decay is starting.
  • Kota Canna GBP post activity — declining engagement signals Google may begin to de-prioritize.
  • MindLift's incremental SoLV gains on each scan — even 2-3 points/month of recovery indicates the boost is working in MindLift's favor.

If by end of June Kota Canna still holds 85%+ SoLV on dispensary-near-me, we escalate to accelerated content + backlink work. If MindLift's SoLV climbs to 25%+ by end of June, the recovery curve is on track and we maintain the defensive cadence.
