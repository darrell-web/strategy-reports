1. Weed-term moat is locked at 100% SoLV across all 4 terms. Do not propose copy changes that pivot AWAY from weed-friendly language — we re-emphasize cannabis/dispensary terms IN ADDITION TO, never instead of, the weed-term foundation.
2. Hours are Sun 10am–8pm · Mon–Sat 9am–10pm storefront · 24/7 delivery every day. Use these in all copy. The 24/7 delivery is the brand differentiator — lead with it, don't bury it.
3. The Dispenza ROI tool ACV is wrong but not catastrophically. Tool shows $50.00; Treez actual = ~$53-58. Account corrects to $55 in admin (ACC-01). Don't cite the inflated number in client comms until corrected.
4. Triple-minority ownership + LGBTQ+ welcome are confirmed via GBP attributes but should be confirmed with Heather (ACC-04) before being amplified externally — we want to ensure the framing matches her preferred voice. Until confirmed, use generic "minority-owned + woman-led" placeholder framing.
5. Credit card acceptance is in GBP attributes but unverified. Account confirms with client (ACC-04) before any copy mentions card acceptance. False claim = chargeback + regulatory risk.
6. Phillipsburg satellite GBP is NOT yet live (per Darrell 2026-05-12). Build the website landing page now to be ready when satellite verification completes. Don't reference the Phillipsburg GBP as "live" in any copy until confirmed.
