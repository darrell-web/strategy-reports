Strategic Plan · May 2026

Big Sur Canna-Botanicals

Defending the lead, closing the gap, and capturing peak-season tourism

Strategic Context

Big Sur Canna enters May 2026 in a strong but nuanced position. The Local SEO engine is operating at its highest historical efficiency — Q1 2026 Google Business Profile conversion rate averaged 92.7%, more than 25 points above the pre-Dispenza baseline. Rank dominance on weed-side terms is locked in at 100% Share of Local Voice. Branded recall is steady. The cannabis-term gap is narrowing.

The structural challenge sits downstream of search visibility: April POS came in at $258K (-16.9% YoY), with a 28-month trend of margin compression and rising discount density. May strategy is therefore framed as "defend and convert" — defend the rank lead, capture peak tourism, and shift the in-store conversion lever to lift AOV without further discount erosion.

Three Strategic Pillars for May

Pillar 1 Defend Weed-Side Dominance & Close Cannabis-Side Gap

Why: Holding 100% SoLV on weed terms is the financial moat. Element 7 Monterey (223 reviews in 2 months) is the most active competitive threat. The cannabis/dispensary side has improving trend signal — capitalize before competitors do.

What we'll do

  • Weekly GBP post cadence (2–3 posts/wk minimum) emphasizing freshness, in-stock items, weekend hours
  • Review-velocity push: budtender QR placards refreshed; staff incentive for verified review asks (target 15+ new reviews in May)
  • Schema deepening on the hidden Brands Page — Product, Offer, and Organization markup
  • FAQ schema on landmark/location pages targeting "cannabis near [landmark]" queries
  • Two new activity-based pages launched: "Cannabis for Memorial Day at Pebble Beach" and "Big Sur Hiking & THC Edibles — Memorial Day Guide"

KPI we'll move

  • Hold "weed" / "weed near me" / "weed dispensary" at SoLV 100%
  • Push at least one cannabis-side term over SoLV 70% (target: "cannabis near me", currently 64.3%)
  • Net new Google reviews in May: 15+

Pillar 2 Capture Peak Tourism Season Demand

Why: May–August is Big Sur's documented peak window. August 2025 saw the highest phone-call volume in the 28-month dataset (117 calls). Tourism intent searches monetize at higher AOV than locals — a margin-positive segment to lean into.

What we'll do

  • Memorial Day weekend (May 23–25) content + GBP post campaign — golf, beach, Highway 1, holiday-weekend pairings
  • AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am tie-in content — "Cannabis Stops Near Pebble Beach", pre-roll tin gift bundles
  • Activity-based blog content rollout (3 pieces): "Cannabis Near Highway 1", "Dispensary Near Monterey Aquarium", "Cannabis Near Cannery Row"
  • "Getting Here" pages for Carmel, Pacific Grove, and Monterey routes with embedded directions, parking notes, and landmark anchors
  • GBP photo refresh featuring outdoor / road-trip / lifestyle imagery (Katie + Chantel coordination)

KPI we'll move

  • Directions requests: 475+ in May (vs 459 in March)
  • Discovery (non-branded) searches: reverse the 6-month decline
  • Branded weekend phone-call volume: track Mon vs Sat/Sun split

Pillar 3 Lift AOV & Defend Margin via Mix Shift (Not Discount)

Why: April discount density at 27.85% is a 28-month high. Gross margin slipped to 42.4% (from 49% in Dec '23). The traffic engine is healthy — the next leg of growth is per-basket efficiency, not promotional depth.

What we'll do

  • WYLD-anchored bundle program — Big Sur's #1 brand by lifetime SKU revenue (42% of top-20 revenue). Test 2-pack and 3-pack edibles bundles with modest 5–10% bundle discount (vs current 27.9% blended discount)
  • Edibles + Cartridge cross-merchandise: tourist-friendly pairings featured in GBP posts, in-store signage, budtender talking points
  • Flower 3.5g (eighth) upsell push — eighths drive higher ticket and have stronger margins than prerolls
  • Loyalty-tier email campaign — re-engage members who have earned but not redeemed points (8.33M earned · 5.72M redeemed lifetime; 2.6M points outstanding represents reactivation opportunity)
  • Coordinate with Big Sur ops team on budtender upsell scripts — pair-with prompts at checkout for the top-5 SKUs

KPI we'll move

  • AOV: $52.51 → recover toward $58 by month-end
  • Discount %: 27.85% → reduce toward 22%
  • Bundle attach rate (new metric to establish in May)
