Big Sur Canna-Botanicals

What every Dispenza department contributes to close the cannabis-side ranking gap and recover discovery search volume

Why This Document Exists

Big Sur's April 2026 GBP impressions dropped 25.7% YoY — concentrated almost entirely in non-branded Discovery searches (-39.7% YoY) while branded Direct searches grew (+11.1% YoY). The diagnosis from the April performance review is confirmed: this is a ranking-driven impression loss, not a market collapse. The two highest-volume April search terms — "dispensary near me" (408) and "dispensary" (407) — are exactly where Big Sur ranks #5 with 60–61% Share of Local Voice.

The May 11 website audit found fixable on-site defects that explain a meaningful portion of that rank gap: missing H1s on five key landing pages, a factually wrong meta description on /about-us, no FAQPage schema anywhere, orphaned tourism content, and a brand featured-list that contradicts actual top-selling SKUs. None of these are speculative — every one is documented at 2026-05-11_website-audit-baseline.md in the client folder.

This document is internal. It tells each Dispenza department what they specifically own this month, with measurable acceptance criteria. The companion May Playbook holds the tactical click-by-click execution.

The Single May Mandate (One Sentence)

Push at least one cannabis-side term ("dispensary near me", "dispensary", or "cannabis near me") above 70% Share of Local Voice by May 31 — by fixing the website defects that block it, accelerating the trends already moving the right direction, and capturing peak tourism intent into the existing high-conversion-rate funnel.

Critical Issues the Whole Team Should Be Aware Of

BLOCKER 1 — /about-us meta description is factually false. Current text in the live Google snippet: "We are a California-based company offering a variety of weed delivery methods to customers in California and select out-of-state locations." Big Sur has zero delivery, single Carmel location, no out-of-state operations (POS Order Source = 100% Normal walk-in). This snippet appears on Google for high-intent branded searches and trains the algorithm for wrong intent. Web/Dev fix this in Week 1 — non-negotiable.
BLOCKER 2 — Five key landing pages have NO H1 element at all. /dispensary-location/carmel, /cannabis-flower, /brands/wyld, /faq, and /contact-us all render with zero H1s. The cannabis-side ranking gap cannot close while pages targeting those queries lack the strongest on-page rank signal. Web/Dev + Content jointly fix in Week 1.
BLOCKER 3 — /cannabis-flower body trains Google for online-ordering intent. Page contains "BUY CANNABIS FLOWER ONLINE" heading + FAQ Qs "Is it legal to buy Cannabis flowers online?" and "Is it safe to buy Marijuana online for recreational use?" Big Sur cannot fulfill online intent. Content owns the rewrite in Week 2.
BLOCKER 4 — FAQ page has 8 questions but no FAQPage schema. Same on /cannabis-flower, /brands/wyld, and /dispensary-location/carmel. Zero AI-overview surface. Web/Dev adds schema; Content audits/extends Q&A bank.
