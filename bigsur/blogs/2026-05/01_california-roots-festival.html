Cannabis Etiquette for the Festival

A quick refresher, because we get asked this every Cali Roots weekend.

You cannot smoke cannabis inside the festival grounds. California Roots Music and Arts Festival is held on Monterey County Fairgrounds property, and smoking cannabis on site violates California public consumption law. Festival security is generally aware and reasonable, but they will ask you to put it out.

You also cannot consume cannabis in our parking lot, on the sidewalk outside our dispensary, or anywhere in public in Monterey. California adult use law restricts cannabis consumption to private property. If you are staying at a private rental or a cannabis friendly hotel, that is your spot.

Edibles, tinctures, and discreet vape carts are the easiest formats for festival weekend for this exact reason. Eat the gummy before you walk in. Take the tincture drops at your rental in the morning. The vape cart, if you bring it, gets used at the rental and not on the fairgrounds.

Bring your government issued ID. You must be twenty one or older to shop at our dispensary, and we check every time. Out of state IDs are welcome. We just need the physical card, not a photo on your phone.
