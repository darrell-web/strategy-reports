What You Cannot Do With Cannabis on Highway 1

California adult use cannabis law gives you a lot of freedom, but the road and the parks have specific rules.

You cannot smoke or vape cannabis in your car on Highway 1, even if you are the passenger. The car is considered a public place under California law when it comes to cannabis.

You cannot consume cannabis at any state park, state beach, or federal land along Highway 1. That includes Andrew Molera State Park, Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park, Garrapata State Park, Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park, Limekiln State Park, Point Lobos State Reserve, and basically every name you have on your stop list. Cannabis on park land is a violation.

You cannot consume cannabis at the scenic turnouts along Highway 1. Those turnouts are technically public road shoulders, and the same public consumption restriction applies.

You cannot consume cannabis at any hotel that does not explicitly allow it. Most major hotels and resorts on the Monterey Peninsula and along Highway 1 do not permit cannabis on property. Check before you book if it matters to you.

You can consume cannabis at:

  • Your private vacation rental, if the host allows cannabis on the property
  • A campsite at a private campground that allows cannabis
  • A private residence (if you know someone along the route, this is the easiest option)

The simple rule: when in doubt, the cannabis stays in the package until you are on private property where the owner says yes.

A Rough Itinerary: Three Days, Highway 1, From Monterey

If you are starting in Monterey and driving south on Highway 1, here is one way to do it.

Day 1: Monterey to Big Sur

Start the morning in Monterey. Visit our dispensary in the morning, when we are not as crowded, and pick up a small selection of products for the rest of your trip (gummies, a couple of prerolls, maybe a tincture, maybe a few cannabis seltzers). Then head south on Highway 1.

Stop at Garrapata State Beach for a short walk. Stop at Bixby Bridge for the obligatory photo. Stop at Point Sur State Historic Park if you have time for the lighthouse tour. Lunch at one of the Big Sur restaurants along Highway 1.

By late afternoon you should be at your rental or campsite in Big Sur. This is where the cannabis you bought in Monterey gets to come out. A gummy on the deck while the sun sets behind the cypress trees is one of the best moments California offers.

Day 2: Big Sur Day

Hike at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park or Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park (McWay Falls is the photo you came for). Eat lunch somewhere along Highway 1 in Big Sur. Remember: no cannabis on park land. Save it for the rental at end of day.

In the evening, back at your rental, your gummies or beverages are waiting. The night sky over Big Sur is one of the clearest you will see in California. Pair it with whatever cannabis you brought from our dispensary, sit on the deck, and watch.

Day 3: Big Sur South to Cambria, or Back North

If you are continuing south, you have a long, beautiful stretch of Highway 1 ahead, and your next licensed cannabis dispensary is in Cambria or San Luis Obispo (about three to four hours of driving from Big Sur). Plan your remaining cannabis supply accordingly.

If you are heading back north, swing through Monterey on your way out. We are happy to put together a takeaway pack so you can carry the weekend home with you (within whatever your local cannabis laws allow).
