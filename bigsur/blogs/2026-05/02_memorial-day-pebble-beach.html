The Pebble Beach Long Weekend in Three Parts

Memorial Day weekend at Pebble Beach is really three different days, and the cannabis that fits each one is different too.

Friday Afternoon: Arrival and Slow Down

You arrived. The drive in was either ninety minutes of Highway One coastal beauty or a three hour stretch from the Bay Area or Los Angeles. Either way, you need to decompress.

Our suggestion for Friday afternoon: something easy and low commitment. A single low dose gummy taken on the patio of your rental or hotel, with a glass of cold water and a view of the ocean. WYLD gummies are popular with our customers for exactly this kind of moment, and the marionberry indica leaning variety is the right call for a slow afternoon. Two and a half to five milligrams per piece is plenty if you have not eaten anything cannabis adjacent in a while.

If you prefer flower over edibles, a small infused preroll shared with whoever you traveled with, while watching the late afternoon light hit the water, is a perfectly respectable way to start a Pebble Beach weekend. Just remember that cannabis consumption is restricted to private property in California. Your rental, yes. The Seventeen Mile Drive turnouts, no.

Saturday: Seventeen Mile Drive and the Long Outdoors

Saturday is the day for actually doing Pebble Beach. Drive Seventeen Mile Drive. Stop at the Lone Cypress. Walk a stretch of the beach at Spanish Bay. If you golf, this is the day for it (more on the golf angle in a separate blog).

For a Saturday outdoors at Pebble Beach, cannabis works best when it is dosed gently and consumed in advance. Specifically:

  • Microdose edibles, 2.5mg to 5mg per piece, taken at the rental about thirty minutes before you head out. You get a soft elevation that pairs perfectly with the cypress trees and the cliff views, without any of the heaviness that would slow you down.
  • A cannabis seltzer at lunch, if your group is grabbing food at a Pebble Beach restaurant that does not serve alcohol. Cannabis seltzers in California are typically 5mg per can, dose in about fifteen minutes, and let you enjoy the afternoon without the alcohol drag.
  • A few drops of tincture under the tongue before a long walk. Three to five milligrams of THC, with or without CBD, depending on whether you want body calm or more clarity.

The point of Saturday cannabis at Pebble Beach is to enhance what is already a beautiful day, not to overwhelm it. Save the bigger doses for the evening.

Sunday: The Whole Coast

Sunday of Memorial Day weekend is for the bigger picture. Maybe you drive south to Big Sur and take Highway One to Bixby Bridge. Maybe you stay closer to Pebble Beach and do another stretch of the Drive. Either way, this is the day to enjoy a real meal, sit somewhere quiet, and let the weekend settle.

We suggest:

  • A slightly bigger edible dose, 5mg to 10mg, taken on the patio of your rental or a private cannabis friendly hotel after dinner. WYLD has a real fruit flavored line that is a longtime customer favorite at our dispensary.
  • A vape cart for whenever you feel like a quick session in private. Pebble Beach evenings can get cool, and a vape on the patio with a jacket on is a perfectly good way to watch the stars come up.
  • A topical cream for sore feet if you walked a lot. Pebble Beach is more walking than most travelers expect.

Memorial Day Cannabis Etiquette at Pebble Beach

A few reminders, because every Memorial Day weekend we field the same questions.

You cannot smoke or vape cannabis on the Pebble Beach grounds. That includes the resort, the lodges, the golf courses, and the public spaces along Seventeen Mile Drive. Pebble Beach is private property in most places and tightly regulated even where it is not. Stick to your rental or a confirmed cannabis friendly accommodation.

You also cannot consume cannabis in public anywhere in California, including the scenic turnouts on Seventeen Mile Drive and the beaches. Edibles, tinctures, and beverages are the legal way to enjoy cannabis in public, but the consumption (the actual chewing, sipping, dropping) should still happen on private property where possible. Once consumed, the effects are personal and discreet.

Driving while impaired by cannabis is illegal, the same as alcohol. If your weekend includes driving Seventeen Mile Drive or heading south to Big Sur, plan your doses around your driving, not the other way around. Or pick the slower mornings for cannabis and let someone else drive when it counts.

Be a respectful guest. Pebble Beach is a luxury resort community. Discreet consumption, no public smoking, no leaving packaging behind. Easy stuff, but it matters.

A Note on Pebble Beach Resorts and Cannabis

The major Pebble Beach resorts and lodges generally do not permit cannabis consumption on property. If you are staying at one of them, plan to consume only in your room with the door closed, or, ideally, look for a private vacation rental that explicitly allows cannabis. Cannabis friendly rentals in the Monterey Peninsula are easy to find on the usual rental platforms with a filter or a direct message to the host.

If you are staying at a private home you own or are renting privately, you are in good shape. Consume freely on your property, store products responsibly out of reach of children, and enjoy the weekend.
