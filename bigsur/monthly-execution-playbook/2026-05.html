How to use this playbook

This is the tactical companion to the May 2026 Strategy. Strategy = what each department owns. Playbook = the exact steps. Every task ID maps to a department commit in the strategy. Acceptance criteria are independently testable — if it can't be checked with a curl, a Rich Results Test, or a Local Falcon scan, it doesn't ship.

Six Guardrails (Read Before Acting)

1. Big Sur has zero online ordering. POS Order Source = 100% Normal walk-in. Never write copy implying online purchase, delivery, or out-of-state shipping. Active fix: /about-us meta + /cannabis-flower body.
2. Hours are 10am–7pm daily. Confirmed across schema, homepage body, location page, FAQ, contact page. Do NOT publish "9–9" hours anywhere (prior Dispenza copy error caught in audit).
3. Discount discipline. April discount density 27.85% is a 28-month high. Default to bundles + loyalty redemption, never new % off promos.
4. SMS cadence ceiling = 1 send/month. Big Sur audience-fatigue posture. SMS subject lines NOT customizable on platform — feature SKUs via body copy.
5. Element 7 watch posture — no reviews arms race. 15/mo target is sustainable; matching 100+/mo would create unsustainable pressure on staff.
6. Cannabis Lounge — pre-build only, no public copy. County approval not landed. Internal keyword research and page templates OK; no published lounge announcements.
