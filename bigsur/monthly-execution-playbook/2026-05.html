Top-Level Guardrails (Read Before Acting)

1. No e-commerce framing. Big Sur has zero online ordering channel (POS Order Source = 100% "Normal"). Never recommend "online order" funnel work in copy or strategy. The "online" reference in the old SEO summary likely meant Special queue pre-orders.
2. Discount discipline. Discount % is at a 28-month high (27.85%). Do NOT propose new % off promos as a default. Lead with bundles, loyalty redemption, and value pairings instead.
3. SMS subject lines are not customizable. Per platform constraint — frame SMS recommendations as which SKUs to feature, not subject-line copy.
4. Cadence discipline. Big Sur's stance is intentionally limited SMS/email cadence to avoid audience fatigue. Loyalty points > deep discounts. Do not propose blast-frequency increases.
5. Element 7 watch. Element 7 Monterey added 223 reviews in 2 months. We are NOT running a reviews arms race. Steady 15+/month review velocity is the target — sustainable, not reactive.
6. Cannabis Lounge — pre-build only. County approval not yet landed. Do NOT pitch lounge SEO content publicly. Internal foundation work (keyword research, schema scaffolding, page templates) is OK in May.
