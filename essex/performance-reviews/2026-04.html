1. Local SEO Wins — The April Story

1.1 Phone Calls Up 67% Year-Over-Year

April delivered 75 phone calls from your Google Business Profile, compared to 45 in April 2025 — a +66.67% year-over-year lift. This is the largest call-volume gain across any Dispenza-tracked metric for Essex in the trailing 6 months. Calls are the highest-intent signal short of a walk-in: every call is a customer with a specific question or product in mind.

1.2 Direction Requests Up 43% Year-Over-Year

April recorded 208 direction requests on Google Maps — up from 145 in April 2025 (+43.45%) and 198 in March (+5.05% MoM). Direction requests are the strongest leading indicator of an in-person visit. The growth here directly translates to walk-in volume, which is 74.3% of all transactions at the store.

2. How Local SEO Translates to Customer Flow + Revenue

📞

Phone Calls → High-Intent Conversations → In-Store Visits

April's 75 phone calls represent customers who took the highest-effort pre-visit action — picking up the phone to ask about a product, pricing, hours, or availability. Phone callers historically convert to in-store visits at materially higher rates than directions-only customers.

At Essex's lifetime average cart value of $33.18 pre-tax, even a 50% phone-to-visit conversion rate translates to ~38 incremental transactions and ~$1,250 in monthly revenue attributable to the call channel alone. The phone-call YoY growth is the closest thing to a guaranteed revenue lift in this report.

📍

Direction Requests → Walk-In Volume

208 directions requests in April. Walk-in transactions are 74.3% of all of Essex's lifetime order mix — directly tied to the customers who use Google Maps to find the store. The 43% YoY directions growth maps directly to the walk-in funnel, which is the primary acquisition channel.

April POS data shows 6,107 units sold across approximately 2,378 distinct orders — that's the in-store flow generated in part by the directions volume.

💻

Website Clicks → Pickup Channel Conversions

April delivered approximately 2,018 website clicks from your GBP profile. The website is your second customer-acquisition channel: it powers the online-ordering-for-pickup flow at essexapothecary.dispensary.shop.

POS data shows 25.6% of all April orders came through the pickup channel — that's roughly 1 in 4 transactions. This is a real, meaningful second channel that the website is feeding. Web clicks are growing slightly YoY (+5.88%) and outpacing impression decline, which signals the website is doing its job of converting profile views into engaged customers.

⭐

Reviews + Brand Search → Repeat Purchase Engine

You ended April with 289 Google reviews at 4.9 stars — best-in-class for the Lynn / North Shore market. Customer reviews repeatedly named your team (Gio, Moses, Ashley, Jess) by first name, which is the strongest possible signal of personal service quality.

POS data confirms this is translating to repeat behavior: 166 customers have made 50+ transactions lifetime, and your loyalty program now boasts an 81.7% lifetime points-redemption rate — the highest engagement rate in Dispenza's entire client portfolio. The brand and product reputation are powering durable retention.

5. High-Level May 2026 Next Steps from Dispenza

The April story is clear: rank dominance is holding, action metrics are growing, and discount discipline is producing real margin lift. The May plan is focused on translating that earned visibility into more new customers and more loyal-customer engagement. Six workstreams:

Close the "Cannabis-Side" Rank Gap

Currently Essex ranks #3-#4 for "dispensary" and "cannabis" (SoLV 52-55%) vs #1 on weed-side. May SEO work targets the same Google Business Profile category/description optimization that closed the gap at peer dispensaries — push at least one of "dispensary near me" or "cannabis near me" above 70% SoLV by month-end.

Lift Loyalty Member Conversion

~942 new customers walk through the door each month. Only ~56 (5.9%) enroll in the loyalty program. Industry typical is 15-25%. Two of your team members — Avery Wayman (253 lifetime enrollments) and Kirstin Snook (203) — are dramatically outperforming the rest of the team on this metric. Their technique becomes the May training model for all budtenders. Target: lift loyalty conversion from 5.9% → 12%+ by month-end.

Drive SMS Opt-In

Only 1.8% of customers (69 of 3,731) have opted into SMS — far below industry standard. SMS is one of the highest-engagement marketing channels available, and at current opt-in volume, it's effectively non-functional for Essex. May tactics include a register-side QR sign-up, a loyalty-points incentive for opting in, and a first-time-customer deal gated on SMS opt-in. Target: 2.5x opt-in volume.

Defend Weed-Side Dominance with Steady Review Velocity

You're at 289 reviews; the goal is sustainable 15+ new reviews per month. Refreshed QR placards, a budtender review-ask script, and tracking weekly — no review arms race, just steady compounding.

Build a Lynn Landing Page on the Website

Your website has 6 neighborhood landing pages (East Lynn, West Lynn, Lynnfield, Peabody, Revere, Saugus) — but no dedicated page for "Lynn, MA" itself. Yet "dispensary lynn ma" is searched 946 times in 6 months. We'll build a dedicated landing page in May to capture this geographically-anchored intent.

Initiate a Brand Co-Promotion Conversation

Glorious Cannabis (parent of Crude Boy, Fire Styxx, Glorious flower, and Commuter) represents 44.5% of your tagged brand revenue and 9 of your top 10 lifetime SKUs. This level of brand concentration is the strongest co-promo opportunity available. Late-May, Dispenza will initiate brand-team outreach to Glorious for a Q2/Q3 summer co-promotional concept.
