Executive Summary

Noire is in an enviable position. 5.0 stars on 688 Google reviews, #1 ranking on every "weed" keyword (95–98% Share of Local Voice), 6 of 7 target keywords now in the top 3 (up from 4 of 7 in March), a 46.4% gross margin, and a stable $63 AOV every month since the Sweed migration in June 2025. The next 60 days are about converting that earned authority into revenue lift.

Four forces shape May

  • "Let's Get Wild" launches Mon, May 11 — a 6-week bundle-driven promo, integrated into 3–4 of the weekly comms (not single-focus, to protect flower momentum).
  • Delivery hard-launches Sun, June 15 — the marketing campaign starts ~June 1 (Memorial Day weekend gives a natural launch ramp).
  • The "everyday low pricing" pivot on flower is showing up in real numbers (March traffic surge, +50% flower units), but the brand promise stays affordable luxury — not low-cost leader.
  • The April 11 Local Falcon scan reveals a single remaining ranking gap: "dispensary" plain at #9 (17.36% SoLV), while every other keyword is top 3. The pattern matches Joyleaf's exactly — the GBP carries "Noire Weed Dispensary" so Google reads "weed dispensary" perfectly but plain "dispensary" weakly. The Joyleaf-proven structural fix (website H1/meta only — no GBP rename) is the highest-leverage ranking move available this month.

May translates each of those into shippable work: Wild Deal cadence + assets, GBP cleanup before delivery flips on, the About Us personal refresh, structural website fixes for "dispensary" rank, and front-of-funnel optimization (the 84% sign-in drop-off is the single largest revenue unlock available).

03

The Strategic Bet for May

May has two parallel bets — one revenue, one ranking. Both are high-leverage and neither blocks the other.

Bet A · Revenue
The fastest path from 4.8% conversion to 5.5–6% is not more traffic — it's fixing the front of the funnel.

84.6% of catalog viewers never sign in. Every Sweed segment, every retention email, every loyalty enrollment starts at sign-in. That single fix outperforms anything else we could do this month.

The supporting bet: the "Let's Get Wild" deal lifts AOV without cannibalizing flower — because we're integrating it into 3–4 weekly comms (not single-focus), pairing edibles with flower bundles, and using it to drive bundle uptake (the $20-eighth → $15 model).

Bet B · Ranking
Make "Noire Dispensary" as recognizable to Google as "Noire Weed Dispensary" already is.

Of 7 target keywords, only one — "dispensary" plain — is outside the top 3. The reason is structural: the GBP carries "Noire Weed Dispensary" in its name, which Google reads strongly for "weed dispensary" but weakly for plain "dispensary." Apothecarium owns 55% SoLV on "dispensary" because their name is just "The Apothecarium Dispensary."

The fix is identical to the Joyleaf playbook (deployed earlier this year and proven): structural website changes — H1, meta titles, schema — that emphasize "Noire Dispensary" without changing the GBP name (which carries suspension risk in NJ). Within 2–4 weeks of crawl, this typically moves the underperforming term meaningfully.

Combined, these two bets cover both immediate revenue and durable ranking. Everything below ladders up to them.
