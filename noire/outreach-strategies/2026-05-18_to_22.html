SECTION 01

The strategic frame

THE WEEK'S HIDDEN CTA

Email is the channel moving the needle. Every deal is a basket-builder. The real ask isn't 30% off — it's contest entry.

Sweed performance 5/1–5/14: email pulled 9.5% average open vs. text 2.5%. Email is the workhorse — text is reinforcement, push is supplementary. All 6 deals are bundle mechanics; the bundle math naturally pushes customers past the $75 Wyld contest threshold. Layer the contest into every send and the deals become the entry mechanism, not just discount offers.

The 3 patterns from the 5/1–5/14 data driving every decision

01

Email is doing the real work

~9.5% avg open vs. text's ~2.5%. Email reaches ~700, text reaches ~620 — and converts ~70 opens per send vs. text's ~19. Lead with email.

02

Text dies on generic themes

"Cart Day Text" hit 0.48%. "Reset The Week" hit 4.99%. Action verbs + specific SKU + price. Never category-only descriptors.

03

Wyld theming is already winning

WYLD Day Email hit 9.80% on 5/13 — top-quartile. The contest gives every send a reason for the customer to convert. Weave it through everything.

Why this Mon–Fri sequence matches the data:
  • Mon 5/18 = Week Launch anchor. Monday email pulled 10.38% open on 5/11 — top performer in the dataset.
  • Wed 5/20 = Wyld Wednesday. Wyld theming hit 9.80% open on Wed 5/13.
  • Thu 5/21 = high-intent pre-weekend slot. Peak Traffic Day email hit 9.27% open on Thu 5/8.
  • Fri 5/22 = closer / last day. Urgency framing capitalizes on weekend buying intent.

SECTION 06

Audience segmentation notes

  • Default broadcast = full email/text/push subscriber list. No exclusions.
  • Wyld-purchaser subsegment (anyone who's previously bought Wyld or Lost Farm products): if Sweed supports a SKU-based segment, prioritize Wednesday's Wyld Wednesday email to this segment with framing like "you've shopped Wyld before — make this the week you go for the year supply." Expected lift based on the "Deals Based on What Customer Has Purchased" performance (10.00% open) is meaningful.
  • High-AOV repeat customers (top 20% spend tier): Tuesday's Premium Picks email should send to this segment first (or with priority send time) since Miss Grass + the Jersey/Sauce Boss/Mac N' Jack strains are premium-positioned. Broaden to full list ~3 hours later.
  • Don't broadcast to the 120 Day Winback audience this week. That workflow (commissioned by Giovanni 5/13) runs its own discount mechanic — avoid stacking discount messaging on dormant customers. Recommend setting Winback start date to Mon 5/26 so it doesn't overlap with this campaign.
