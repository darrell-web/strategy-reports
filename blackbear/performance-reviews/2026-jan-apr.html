Local SEO Performance · Jan to April 2026

Black Bear Dispensary

Google Business Profile performance across all 5 PA locations + the Winona MN expansion. Per-store sections below.

Executive Summary

Scope note: Black Bear does not currently share POS data with Dispenza, so this report focuses entirely on Google Business Profile discovery traffic and bottom funnel interactions (calls · directions · website clicks · chat clicks). KPIs are GBP-only; revenue / AOV / transaction counts are not in scope.

Jan-April 2026 was a mixed quarter across the Black Bear portfolio. Four of five PA locations saw year-over-year declines on discovery impressions (profile views down 25 to 44%) and on Business Profile interactions (down 11 to 45%). The exception is Meadville, which posted +26.9% YoY interactions and +46.2% YoY calls — the only PA store with positive YoY action growth in the period. The new Winona MN location went live April 13 and delivered 2,698 interactions in its first 18 days.

The core pattern is consistent across PA: discovery impressions softened materially (likely a combination of seasonal cannabis-search softening + algorithm reweighting) but the customers Google did send converted at high rates — Meadville at 48.9% conversion, Clarion at 42.6%, Grove City at 38.4%. The Pittsburgh-area stores (Bridgeville at 18.7%, Regent Square at 16.8%) show lower per-impression conversion, which is the highest-leverage optimization target for May.

Cross-Portfolio Observations

1. Discovery softening is portfolio-wide (PA)

All 4 PA stores with YoY data show profile-view declines of 25% to 44%. Searches are down 30% to 48%. The pattern is too consistent across rural and urban PA to be location-specific — likely drivers include a broader cannabis-search market softening (similar pattern seen at Essex Apothecary in MA), Google's recent removal of "Offers delivery" attribute at most PA stores, and the standard Q1-to-Q2 seasonal pattern. Worth a Local Falcon scan baseline across all 5 PA locations to track ranking position alongside the impression decline.

2. Brand recall is strong (60-65% branded share)

Across all 4 mature PA stores, the top 10-12 search queries are ~60-65% branded ("black bear dispensary" + variants). This is dramatically higher than typical Dispenza-portfolio clients (most are 2-25% branded share, dominated by Discovery). Black Bear's multi-year compounding has produced exceptional brand recall — worth treating as a real asset and continuing brand-awareness investment.

3. Description / title inconsistency at 4 of 5 PA stores

GBP titles correctly use "Hemp Dispensary" across all 5 PA stores (compliant with the payment-processor rule). But the descriptions at Clarion · Grove City · Meadville · Regent Square all use "Black Bear Weed Dispensary" in the opening line. Only Bridgeville's description is clean. Recommend a portfolio-wide description rewrite to align the "Hemp" naming across both title and description fields. Same fix needed at Winona MN (with the GBP rename).

4. Urban vs rural conversion gap

Rural PA stores (Clarion 42.6% · Meadville 48.9% · Grove City 38.4%) convert at 2-3× the rate of Pittsburgh-area stores (Bridgeville 18.7% · Regent Square 16.8%). The rural audience is more action-prone; the urban audience browses more without acting. The single highest-leverage May optimization is lifting Bridgeville + Regent Square conversion through photo refresh, GBP description rewrite, and post cadence intensification.

5. Service-options "Offers delivery" removal

Google removed the "Offers delivery" attribute from Clarion + Grove City (confirmed in back-office tabs). May have also impacted Meadville and Regent Square (some service-options inconsistencies observed). This change correlates with the YoY discovery decline timing — worth confirming with client whether delivery is actually offered and re-adding if applicable, or updating descriptions to reflect the no-delivery reality.

6. DEI ownership attribute inconsistency

Bridgeville surfaces 5 ownership identity attributes (women + small + disabled + Latino + veteran). Regent Square surfaces 4 (Latino + disabled + women + small — NO veteran). Clarion has 2. Grove City + Meadville have 1 each (veteran only). If the same owner-of-record applies portfolio-wide, all PA stores should match Bridgeville's 5-attribute set. Recommend a single audit + portfolio-wide update.
