May 2026 — defend the wins, close the one gap

April delivered Joyleaf's strongest local SEO position yet. May's plan is two-part: defend the eight #1 rankings (especially the three 100% SoLV positions) and close the single remaining gap on "dispensary near me."

1 · Close the "dispensary near me" gap

The highest-volume query that surfaces Joyleaf (1,571 monthly searches) is also the one term where Joyleaf currently sits #4 behind Breakwater Dispensary in Roselle Park (46.28% SoLV vs Joyleaf's 33.06%). Closing this gap unlocks the last keyword in the set.

  • Add citation work + local backlinks targeting "dispensary near me" intent.
  • Increase GBP photo cadence to 20+/month (current pace below this) — Google's local-pack algorithm weights GBP freshness heavily for "near me" queries.
  • Target Breakwater specifically for monthly Local Falcon checks — track whether the gap is closing month over month.

2 · Defend the eight #1 rankings

  • Maintain review velocity (target 60/month) — review count + freshness directly support the SoLV positions.
  • Weekly GBP posts with commercial-intent keywords (e.g., "weed dispensary near you," "cannabis delivery Roselle") to keep the listing fresh.
  • GBP photo cadence: 20 photos/month minimum, mix of products, storefront, customer interactions, and seasonal promos.

3 · Sustain the +66% YoY phone call growth

Phone Calls jumped from 192 → 318 YoY (+66%). That's a real signal of customer intent. May priorities:

  • Audit call response time + script quality — high call volume only produces results if the team picks up and handles inquiries well. Worth a 30-minute review with the store team on call handling.
  • Monitor monthly call volume through May to validate whether the +66% YoY trajectory holds or accelerates. Phone Calls are the highest-intent GBP action — sustaining them is the goal.

4 · Build on the branded search momentum

Branded queries ("joyleaf...") generated 943 searches in April. The growth path for branded demand is community visibility:

  • Continue local partnership content (the 5 NJ towns: Elizabeth, Roselle, Roselle Park, Union, Springfield) — geographic queries with "nj" or specific town names show up consistently in the top 20.
  • Customer photo reviews reinforce brand recognition in search results.
The strategic position heading into May: Joyleaf has the strongest local SEO footprint in the Roselle / Union County area — #1 on 8 of 9 tracked keywords, with three at 100% SoLV. This is the kind of competitive moat that protects market position even as the NJ cannabis market matures and total search volume softens. The challenge ahead is consistency, not breakout — defend the position, close the one gap, and keep driving GBP engagement (clicks, directions, calls).
