What This Means

  • Local search is doing the heavy lifting. 342 direction requests + 156 phone calls + 511 website clicks in April — that's people finding you and acting. The GBP-driven funnel is feeding the store.
  • Cart growth is the standout win. Average cart up $3.38 vs baseline. That alone — at April's customer volume — is worth roughly $4,200 in monthly revenue at constant transaction count.
  • March was the high-water mark, April held the line. March was a breakout month (1,331 customers, $88k revenue, 1,094 actions). April softened slightly but stayed above Jan/Feb. That's a healthy stabilization, not a reversal.

Opportunities We're Watching

  • "Dispensary" and "cannabis" terminology. SOLV is in the 40% range vs. 100% on "weed"-family terms. There is real upside to closing that gap because some customers default to "dispensary" or "cannabis" instead of "weed."
  • Medical patient retention. The medical segment held customer count but softened on revenue — worth a closer look at retention or visit frequency among medical regulars.
  • Pickup / online ordering. Pickup is still small ($885 in April / 16 orders) but doubled month-over-month. Worth nudging via GBP and the website.
