PILLAR 03

3Cross-Border V2 Pilot (Ontario / Ohio)

Lenox sits ~30 mi NE of Detroit on Gratiot Ave (M-3) heading toward Port Huron. From Port Huron, Ontario is across the Blue Water Bridge; from I-94, Ohio is ~2 hrs south. Lenox is the chain's untested border-market opportunity. Mirror the Saline (Ohio V2) and New Buffalo (IL/IN V2) models.

Pilot architecture

  • Audience source: Sweed POS export filter for non-Michigan ZIP codes among Lenox customers. Target: Ontario postal codes (Sarnia, London, Windsor area), Ohio ZIPs (Toledo metro, Northeast Ohio).
  • Upload: AIQ V2 audience module — same workflow as Saline/Ohio + New Buffalo/IL-IN.
  • Promo: $100-min spend wallet load (vs New Buffalo's $150-min — Lenox is a smaller market, lower threshold) with cross-border-traveler messaging.
  • Cadence: Bi-weekly (every other week) for May. If May shows traction, increase to weekly in June.
  • Cannabis-compliance note: Ontario Canadian customers buying in Michigan is legal in MI; Ohio cross-border is legal in MI. The promotion is delivered to MI store, not exported. Compliance OK.
