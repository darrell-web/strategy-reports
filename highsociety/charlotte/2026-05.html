The Strategic Bet for May

May 2026 Strategic Bet
Charlotte's review-velocity advantage is the chain's most valuable unidentified asset. Diagnose it. Replicate the driver to BR / Saline / Lenox. Close the Weedmaps gap. Add Lansing metro positioning to the already-best-in-chain description. Don't break what's working.

Charlotte's review-velocity advantage — diagnostic hypotheses

Charlotte earns reviews at 5× the chain average. Whatever's driving it — store-staff process, customer-base demographic, or a tooling difference — finding the cause is worth more than any other single May tactic at any store. The diagnostic is the highest-priority item.

HypothesisHow to test
H1: Charlotte staff has a tighter checkout review-prompt scriptCompare front-desk + budtender scripts. Visit Charlotte vs. Big Rapids for direct observation. Talk to GMs.
H2: Charlotte runs a different review-acquisition kiosk / SMS / receipt-insert comboAudit the post-purchase flow at each store. Map AIQ post-visit campaign timing per store. Charlotte's post-visit SMS may fire faster or with a different incentive.
H3: Charlotte's customer-base demographic skews more review-active (younger / more app-engaged / Lansing-metro university spillover)POS data segmentation — repeat-customer rate, average visit frequency, AVO distribution. If Charlotte's customer mix differs materially, that's the driver and it's not replicable directly.
H4: Charlotte has been actively running a review-incentive promo that other stores haven'tAudit AIQ campaign history at Charlotte vs. other stores. Look for "leave a review for X off" type campaigns.
H5: Charlotte's staff has lower turnover + better review-response cadence (positive feedback loop)HR check on staff tenure. Review-response audit on Charlotte's existing reviews.

If H1-H2-H4-H5 are the drivers, the model is replicable. If H3 is the only driver, the gap stays. Either answer is valuable.

2Weedmaps Profile Rebuild · The Big P0

28 Weedmaps reviews vs 1,067 Google reviews is a 38× ratio (vs chain average ~3×). Charlotte's Weedmaps is severely under-fed. May's biggest individual-store opportunity is closing this gap.

Weedmaps audit checklist

  1. Profile completeness: banner, logo (high-res, license # visible), hours match GBP exactly, NAP exact-match (515 Lansing St)
  2. Photos: ≥20 across categories (interior, exterior, products, team) — currently likely <5 given the under-development
  3. Description copy: use the new GBP description (Pillar 01) — Weedmaps allows ~750 chars
  4. Deals: ≥3 active deals at all times, mirroring Charlotte's GBP cadence
  5. Menu sync: verify Sweed/Dutchie POS → Weedmaps integration is firing within 24 hrs of POS update. If broken, this alone explains the under-development.
  6. Review acquisition: add Weedmaps to the post-purchase review-request flow — receipt insert + AIQ post-visit campaign should include "Leave us a review on Weedmaps too!"
  7. Existing review responses: respond to all 28 existing Weedmaps reviews within 48 hrs (chain-wide standard)

Why Charlotte's Weedmaps under-development matters

In Michigan, Weedmaps is the #1 cannabis-vertical discovery platform. A 28-review profile vs. 489 at Birch Run means Charlotte is essentially invisible in Weedmaps' Charlotte-area discovery layer. Closing the gap to ~150 reviews over 6 months would add an estimated 200-400 incremental visits/month from Weedmaps direct traffic.
