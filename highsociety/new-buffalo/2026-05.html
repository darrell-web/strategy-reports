PILLAR 03

3VIP Bulk-Buyer Cohort Formalization

The master summary doc explicitly names "VIP bulk flower and vape case buyers" as a New Buffalo customer segment. The AIQ calendar's $150-min wallet drops are an early-stage attempt to address this. May formalizes the cohort definition + creates a dedicated VIP stream.

Cohort definition (proposed)

  • Customer who has spent ≥$500 in last 90 days (POS-derived), OR
  • Customer with ≥3 visits in last 60 days each >$200, OR
  • Customer with bulk-flower (>1oz transaction) or case-vape (>6 unit) purchase in last 90 days

VIP-stream campaigns (proposed)

CampaignCadenceChannel

This separates VIP behavior from the chain's standard win-back / Power Hour / Happy Hour cohorts. VIP customers shouldn't be receiving the same generic 50/30/25 win-back push as a 60-day-dormant casual customer.
