Why this plan will hit its KPIs

Mount Pleasant has the chain's most architecturally distinctive storefront, healthy reviews (4.8★/596 Google + 4.8★/148 Weedmaps), CMU adjacency on the city's main commercial corridor, and a noon traffic peak that confirms the university-cohort hypothesis. What it lacks is GBP attribute completeness — Mount Pleasant is missing 4-5 chain-wide auto-applied attributes that every other store has. Filling those + surfacing CMU in copy + tightening AIQ for noon traffic is the May path.

🚨 Mount Pleasant is the chain's least-complete listing. Missing attributes: Accessibility (wheelchair entrance + parking — every other chain store has these auto-applied) · Recycling (No electronics — chain-wide auto-fill) · "Onsite services available" (chain-wide rollout) · "No delivery" (chain-pattern at older stores) · Parking attribute. Proof point #1 · CMU adjacency = university-cohort gold

Central Michigan University (~16K students + faculty) is on Mission Street — Mount Pleasant's main thoroughfare. The dispensary is on N Mission. Description: 0 references. Adding "minutes from CMU" + university-cohort phrasing is a low-effort high-leverage rewrite. The popular-times noon peak confirms the demographic is real.

Proof point #2 · Distinctive storefront = brand-marketing asset

Red brick + dark green awning + prominent vertical "HIGH SOCIETY CANNABIS" signage. Most architecturally distinctive of all 8 stores. A real visual asset for hyperlocal page hero imagery + local press feature angles. Other stores' photo cadence should rotate to the kind of distinctive shots Mount Pleasant lends itself to naturally.

Proof point #3 · Customer reviews call out the vibe

"Music choice was on point" + "I love the atmosphere and the weed smell lol" + "Best prices in town, super friendly staff" — actual review snippets. The vibe-driven retail experience is real and unleveraged in description copy.

PILLAR 01

1Same-Day Attribute Population + NAP Fix

Day 1-5 fixes (each task < 5 min in GBP)

  1. Accessibility: Add "Has wheelchair accessible entrance" + "Has wheelchair accessible parking lot." Confirm on-site if needed.
  2. Recycling: Add "No electronics recycling." (Chain-wide auto-fill standard.)
  3. "Onsite services available": Add to Service options. Chain-wide rollout standard.
  4. "No delivery": Add to Service options. Chain pattern at older stores.
  5. Parking: Verify on-site reality. Most likely "Free parking lot" given freestanding-building context. Populate.
  6. NAP canonicalization: Set internal Location tab address + service area to consistent "North Mission Street" + "Mount Pleasant." Update Weedmaps + Leafly + Yelp + BBB + Bing + Apple to match.
  7. Facebook URL: Confirm FB page exists + has admin access. Add the URL to GBP Contact tab Social profiles. If Google removed it previously, investigate root cause first (NAP drift on FB page is most common reason).
