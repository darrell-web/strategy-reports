The Strategic Bet for May

May 2026 Strategic Bet
Make Saline the chain's "Ann Arbor's cannabis Supercenter" by leaning into the format modifier with tangible specifics + adjacent-metro positioning + I-94 corridor visibility. The Pit Stop reinstatement + parking attribute fix are same-day hygiene; the Ann Arbor metro framing is the rank lever.

The chain has 8 stores across multiple Michigan markets. Saline is the only one in Ann Arbor metro reach. That position alone — combined with the Supercenter format modifier nobody else uses except East Lansing — gives Saline a unique-in-chain category to own. Description copy that names the position, secondaries that match the category, and hyperlocal pages that capture the metro spillover all reinforce the same bet.

Why "Supercenter" is the lever

Only 2 of 8 chain stores use the "Supercenter" modifier (Saline + East Lansing). It's a unique-in-chain format positioning — but it only works if the description makes it tangible. Saying "we're a Supercenter" without proof is empty marketing; saying "Washtenaw County's biggest cannabis selection by SKU count" is a defensible claim that customers can verify in-store. That single phrase transforms the format modifier from generic to defensible differentiation.

PILLAR 02

2GBP Attribute & Category Optimization

Three discrete fixes. Each takes <15 minutes in the GBP dashboard. Combined expected lift: +5 to +12 SoLV over 4–8 weeks.

Fix 2a · Verify "Paid parking lot" → likely correct to "Free parking lot"

The "Paid parking lot" attribute was Google-auto-applied. Saline is a freestanding building next to Busch's Fresh Food Market (free retail parking), not a paid-lot configuration. East Lansing — same context (freestanding next to Costco) — shows "Free parking lot" explicitly. Verify on-site reality with Saline staff; if the lot is free, edit the attribute immediately. Inaccurate parking attributes deter visits + send confused-customer signals.

Fix 2b · Secondary category swap (chain-staggered)

The chain template has 8 secondaries identical across all 6 stores audited. Two slots are weak/redundant:

Stagger across the chain — don't apply to all 8 stores on the same day (Google may flag coordinated low-quality edits). Suggested cadence: 2 stores/week × 4 weeks. Saline is recommended for Week 2 of May.

Saline currently lists only ZIP 48176 as service area. The chain-pattern is single-ZIP for most stores, but East Lansing already pioneered multi-area coverage (Lansing + East Lansing). Saline should follow:
