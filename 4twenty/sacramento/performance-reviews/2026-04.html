SECTION 04 — GBP ATTRIBUTE CONFLICTS

Two CX leaks queued for owner approval

1. "Offers delivery" still set despite May 2025 discontinuation. POS data shows Sacramento last delivered 71 orders in May 2025 — operationally decommissioned for 12 months. GBP attribute hasn't been toggled. The 15-city service area (Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova, Citrus Heights, etc.) was sized for that delivery zone. Customers searching delivery in greater Sacramento see Abatin in results, click through, and bounce. Toggle "Offers delivery" OFF; set "No delivery" as negative attribute. Revisit service area.
2. Description-vs-attribute contradiction on curbside pickup. Sacramento's GBP description says: "Enjoy the convenience of online ordering and curbside pickup for a seamless experience." Back-end Service Options explicitly set: "No curbside pickup" as negative attribute. Customers who drive in expecting curbside will be disappointed. Either turn the attribute ON or remove the description claim. Need a confirm from ops on what's actually offered.
