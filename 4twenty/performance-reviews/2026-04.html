The April easing-up pattern — uneven across stores

WeHo held discipline across April

Pulled discount BACK 9.8%, held margin flat at 54.4%, drew down inventory 7.8%, grew AOV 2.9%. The only store of the three that improved unit economics. Premium positioning was the playbook — and it worked.

Sacramento + Oakland traded margin for volume

Sacramento discount $ up 30.6% to drive +13.3% units; margin gave up 2.3 pts. Oakland discount $ up 18.5% to drive +1.3% units; margin gave up 1.3 pts. Inventory built up 15–20% at both stores. Combined ~$33K of extra inventory cost on hand at end of April. If May doesn't see a planned drawdown, working capital is the next question.

The trade in dollars

Chain-wide April: ~$23,500 in incremental net sales bought with ~$19,300 in incremental discounts. That's a 1.2:1 ratio. Volume growth at margin cost is a marginal trade — not a great one. The May question for the team: at Sacramento and Oakland, what happens to volume if we pull back to WeHo-style discount discipline? If volume holds, the April push was unnecessary margin spend.
