What's coming in May

May is focused on strengthening your local SEO foundations across the chain and amplifying your Google Business Profile presence at each of the three locations.

We're delivering a coordinated effort: one round of website enhancements that benefits all three stores, plus location-specific GBP work tailored to each market and each brand legacy.

Themes for the month

  1. Bring each location's story forward. Phytologie's Bay Area heritage in Oakland, Abatin Wellness's path-to-wellness lineage in Sacramento, and LAPCG's two-decade legacy in West Hollywood are all SEO and brand-trust assets. We're putting them front-and-center.
  2. Brand-carry visibility. The premium brands you carry — Stiiizy, Wyld, Camino, Sherbinskis, Raw Garden, Khalifa Kush, Mary's Medicinals, and more — are search magnets. We're weaving them into your website pages and your GBP rhythm so brand-curious shoppers find you first.
  3. Polished GBP cadence. Three posts a week per location, refreshed descriptions, expanded Q&A inventory, and a tighter service-area picture for each store.

Chain-wide enhancements

One effort. All three stores benefit at once.

We're shipping a single round of website improvements to 4twentymarket.com that benefits Oakland, Sacramento, and West Hollywood simultaneously:

  • Refreshed page foundations so Google and search-aware AI tools can clearly read each location's identity, address, hours, and offerings
  • Location-specific narrative copy added to each page, telling the story of the location's roots and community connection
  • Brand-carry highlights woven into the on-page experience
  • Local-area context — neighborhoods, nearby landmarks, and community references that ground each page in its market
  • Regulatory polish — your CA license numbers formatted to match official state records on every page

This is the highest-leverage SEO work available this month and the work compounds: every location benefits at the same time.

Why this combination

Each store sits in a different competitive position — Oakland is in defense mode, Sacramento has room to expand on key search terms, and West Hollywood is on a clear upward trajectory. The May plan respects those differences while taking advantage of the chain's shared website to ship enhancements once that benefit all three locations.

Pairing the website foundation work with consistent, branded GBP cadence is the single most reliable lever for moving local rankings and turning search visibility into walk-in traffic. That's where May's effort lives.
