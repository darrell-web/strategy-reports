§ 1

Executive Summary

4Twenty Market is a 3-LLC partnership umbrella — Abyz, LLC (Oakland · Phytologie), Migliore, LLC (Sacramento · Abatin Wellness), and Ovest, LLC (West Hollywood · LAPCG) — operating as one customer-facing brand. Each store sits in a completely different competitive position.

Oakland dominates (95–100% SoLV across all 5 keywords; the leverage is conversion not ranking). Sacramento splits (dominant on "weed" terms, structural gap on "dispensary"/"cannabis"). West Hollywood is gaining (+11.57 SoLV on "weed" — largest single-keyword move in the WeHo data).

But every page on 4twentymarket.com shares the same template gaps: no meta description, no schema.org JSON-LD, no body copy, and the word "cannabis" appears nowhere in any H1, H2, H3, or <title>. Fixing the template once fixes all three stores at the same time. This is the highest-leverage move available this month — the same Joyleaf/Noire structural play, scaled to chain.

May has three parallel bets

Bet A · Chain-wide ranking
Ship the template fix. One engineering effort that benefits all 3 stores at once.

Add meta descriptions, LocalBusiness JSON-LD with alternateName arrays, location-specific body copy, and "cannabis" keyword integration across all 3 location pages. Within 2–4 weeks of crawl, this typically moves underperforming terms meaningfully. Sacramento needs this most; Oakland and WeHo benefit too.

Bet B · P0 GBP cleanup
Resolve documented CX harm. Three items each have direct evidence they're costing customers right now.

(a) Oakland "Offers delivery" attribute — the 3★ Google review citing "no delivery" is direct CX evidence. (b) Same on Sacramento (decommissioned May 2025 per POS). (c) The WeHo duplicate "4twenty Market" 2.9★ GBP listing dragging brand perception. All three ship Week 1.

Bet C · Oakland visibility → revenue
At 95–100% SoLV in a 1-mi grid, the marginal SEO ROI is low.

Oakland's leverage shifts to GBP description rewrite, Posts cadence emphasizing in-stock brands (Stiiizy, Wyld, Camino, Raw Garden, Khalifa Kush — all confirmed in Oakland photo gallery), first-time customer review prompts. Same conversion lift applies to WeHo (smaller but trajectory is right).
